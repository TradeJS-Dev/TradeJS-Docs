Use TradeJS to write trading strategies in TypeScript, test them on historical
data, and run them on infrastructure you operate.

2. [Run your first backtest](./getting-started/first-backtest).
3. [See examples](./examples) or [write your first strategy](./strategies/authoring/typescript-strategy-step-by-step).
4. [Prepare a strategy for live trading](./getting-started/run-strategy-in-production) when you have a tested configuration.
